About Geet

Where Science Meets Style

Est. Shropshire

The Wenlock Wardrobe has been a beloved part of the Shropshire community for over two decades — a multi-generational boutique where fashion meets individuality. At the heart of it is Geet, a specialist in colour analysis, body shape styling and wardrobe transformation.

Trained in the science of undertone theory and seasonal colour frameworks, Geet brings both analytical precision and warm, personal guidance to every consultation. Whether you're navigating Western fashion, cultural dress, or a blend of both — her approach celebrates every woman's unique palette.

Based in Shropshire for in-person sessions, Geet travels nationally for group consultations and offers virtual colour analysis to clients worldwide — so wherever you are, your best colours are within reach.
